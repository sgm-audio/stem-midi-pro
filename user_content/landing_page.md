# Stem+MIDI Pro

Studio-grade stem separation + editable MIDI drafts.
Not magic—just math that respects your craft.

## ✓ What You Get
- Phase-coherent guitar/bass stems (24-bit WAV)
- DAW-ready MIDI with per-note confidence scores (CC#127)
- Runs **locally on your own machine** — CPU-only, no cloud, no GPU. A $200 i5 laptop is enough (expect ~30–60 s of processing per minute of audio).

## For Producers Who Need Drafts, Not Promises

### How It Works
1. **Upload** your audio file (WAV/FLAC/MP3, <10 min)
2. **AI Processing** separates stems and transcribes to MIDI with confidence scoring
3. **Review & Edit** low-confidence notes in your own DAW — they're tagged with CC#127 so they're easy to find
4. **Export** a package with stems, MIDI, and a transparent processing report

### Quality You Can Trust
- See exactly what the AI is confident about (and what it's not)
- Low-confidence notes tagged with MIDI CC#127 for easy DAW identification
- Every job ships with `processing_report.json`: SI-SDR estimate, phase coherence, confidence, quality tier
- Your audio is processed in memory and deleted after 24h

### Perfect For
- Creating backing tracks from existing recordings
- Generating MIDI arrangements for remixing or arrangement
- Transcribing guitar/bass parts for notation or learning
- Quick demos and idea generation
